## **⚠️ Risk Mitigation**

### **Technical Risks**
- **API Rate Limits**: Implement caching and rate limiting
- **Performance Issues**: Regular monitoring and optimization
- **Security Vulnerabilities**: Regular security audits
- **Browser Compatibility**: Cross-browser testing

### **Business Risks**
- **Scope Creep**: Clear requirements and change management
- **Timeline Delays**: Buffer time and parallel development
- **Quality Issues**: Comprehensive testing strategy
- **Client Expectations**: Regular communication and demos
